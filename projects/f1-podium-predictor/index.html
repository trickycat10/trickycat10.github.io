F1 Podium Predictor

I built a model that ranks drivers by their chances of finishing on the podium, using historical race data from FastF1 and Jolpica.

Python · LightGBM · Streamlit

Working with race history

I used rolling features based on earlier races so later results could not leak into predictions.

From rankings to probabilities

LightGBM LambdaRank produces driver rankings. I used 10,000-run Plackett–Luce simulations to turn those rankings into podium odds for a Streamlit dashboard.

Evaluation

Across 951 driver entries from 2024–2025, the evaluation produced an NDCG@3 ranking score of 0.887 and a reported podium hit rate of 82.5%.
